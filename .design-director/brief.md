# Project brief

- Site model: corporate landing with a development showcase, not an online store.
- Experience tier: immersive, with one primary 3D interaction and at most two supporting motion patterns.
- Audience: technical directors, UAV product teams and companies commissioning electronics development.
- Business goal: prove engineering competence with real boards, then move the visitor to a concise project inquiry.
- Source boundary: use only confirmed company services and client-approved boards. Do not invent metrics, clients, certifications or production capacity.
- Current task: rethink the visual and interaction system before touching the approved implementation.
- Required interaction: real board visible immediately or reached through a deliberately selected cinematic transition; full rotation, reliable zoom, readable lighting from every angle and a lightweight mobile fallback.
- Required restraint: motion must explain the product, not create an arcade-like interface.
