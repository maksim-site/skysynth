# UX foundation

## Content hierarchy

1. What the company develops.
2. A real board that proves the work.
3. Other developments and their factual descriptions.
4. Competencies and engineering process.
5. Project inquiry.

## Interaction rules

- The board itself is the primary affordance; a short hint appears once and then recedes.
- Drag rotates on a full sphere; wheel/pinch zooms within safe bounds; double-click/tap resets the view.
- Keyboard and visible controls remain available without building a large control bar.
- Pointer interaction pauses idle motion immediately; idle motion resumes only after a short delay.
- Reduced-motion mode disables auto-rotation, particles and cinematic transitions while keeping inspection usable.
- Loading begins with a poster rendered from the same 3D scene and camera, preventing a jump when WebGL becomes ready.

## Conversion rules

- Keep one primary CTA in the hero and repeat it only after proof/process sections.
- Do not gate the 3D experience behind a CTA.
- Technical labels support the scene but never cover the board.
