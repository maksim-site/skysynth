# Reference board

## Direct category references

- Flywoo: useful for product taxonomy and the expectation of strong product imagery; avoid copying its dense ecommerce shell.
- SpeedyBee: useful for catalog/support structure and technical product grouping; avoid its conventional store presentation.

## Adjacent interaction references

- Horizon 3D Prototypes: immediate interactive hero, scroll-synchronised product, spatial carousel and annotations.
- Emersya Electronics: all-angle inspection, zoom, interactive annotations and exploded/internal component views.
- Nothing product pages: the physical product is treated as the centre of the visual system, with editorial UI supporting it.
- XO3D: one CAD source, one lighting/material language across stills, motion and interactive output; each motion sequence has a specific explanatory job.

## Transfer rules

- Transfer the product-first hierarchy, not another brand's chrome.
- Transfer consistent camera, material and light direction across still and interactive states.
- Use one signature interaction; supporting effects must remain quiet.
- Do not use generated video for arbitrary user-controlled board switching. Generated video may be used as a locked-camera ambient background plate only.
