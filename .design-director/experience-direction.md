# Experience direction

## Candidate A — Levitation bench

The hero opens directly in a live 3D scene. A real board floats a short distance above a precision inspection base. The user can rotate and zoom immediately. Subtle contact shadow, a restrained field ring and sparse particles establish depth.

- Composition: centred product stage; compact copy anchored to a side rail rather than a generic 50/50 split.
- Idle motion: 4–7 px vertical travel over 5.5–7 seconds; under 1 degree of pitch/roll; optional very slow yaw that stops on interaction.
- Strength: fastest proof that the site is built around real engineering work.
- Risk: highest first-load and rendering demand.

## Candidate B — Inspection rail

The first screen is a cinematic, restrained company statement. The next screen is a wide physical rail carrying several boards. Arrow, drag or wheel input slides the whole rail, locks one carrier into the inspection position and reveals its factual description. A small action opens that board into an all-angle viewer in place.

- Composition: full-width documentary hero followed by a horizontal showroom; no right-side hero card.
- Motion: fast mechanical translation with short settle, depth parallax and a locked-camera ambient background plate.
- Strength: best way to show the breadth of developments without making an ecommerce grid.
- Risk: the first screen itself is less immediately interactive.

## Candidate C — Seamless materialisation

The current premium lab scene remains the first impression. A real still render sits on the stand. On click or scroll it performs a combined yaw/pitch rotation with a speed ramp. At the thinnest edge-on moment, a matched live GLB replaces the still under brief motion blur and lands in a fully interactive state.

- Composition: full-bleed photographic stage with overlay copy and the board as the only interaction target.
- Motion: controlled 220–280 degree turn, accelerated middle, soft deceleration, then free orbit.
- Strength: preserves the strongest part of the current design and creates the most cinematic reveal.
- Risk: demands exact matching of camera, scale, lighting and colour between poster and WebGL.

## Recommendation

Choose Candidate A as the main hero system. Use a simplified Candidate B later as the developments section. Do not also add Candidate C as a second hero spectacle; it would compete with the immediate 3D interaction.
