# Project framing

## Core promise

Electronics for UAVs from circuit design to a production-ready result.

## Selected site model

- Current recommendation: B2B corporate landing with a development showcase and inquiry conversion.
- Evidence: the supplied brief describes custom engineering, PCB and embedded-software development, prototypes, testing, documentation and production support. The supplied board descriptions explain functions but contain no SKU, price, availability, order quantity, warranty, payment or delivery terms.
- Commerce status: unconfirmed. Do not add cart, checkout, payment or delivery until the company explicitly confirms that one or more boards are public serial products sold on standard terms.
- If serial products exist: expand later into a hybrid corporate + catalog model. Product pages may use `Запросить цену` or `Запросить поставку` before full ecommerce.

## Primary conversion

- Primary: `Обсудить проект` → a short qualified inquiry.
- Secondary: direct Telegram, email and phone contacts.
- Form: name, company, contact, task type and a free-form task description. File upload is optional only if the company is ready to receive technical briefs safely.

## Page job

1. Establish a premium, technically credible first impression.
2. Let the visitor inspect a real development instead of viewing a decorative mockup.
3. Show the range of boards and the engineering process without turning the page into a product store.
4. Convert interest into a project discussion.

## Invariants

- Real client models remain the visual source of truth.
- Dark precision-lab character remains the primary art direction; light mode is a genuine metrology-lab variant, not a color inversion.
- Primary CTA wording remains direct and calm.
- Existing approved content is preserved until the client supplies additions.
